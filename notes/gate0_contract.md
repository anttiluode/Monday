# Gate 0 contract

FA-BSS0 passes only if, across multiple seeds and multiple non-degenerate 2x2 mixing matrices:

1. rewarded structural remodeling improves frozen-anatomy target-source purity relative to the same bootstrap anatomy;
2. improvement exceeds reward-shuffle/random-mutation controls under matched mutation budget;
3. the effect appears on fresh source realizations;
4. independently probed terminal-to-soma transfer coefficients account for the improvement;
5. output gain remains non-trivial;
6. the result is not explained by simple spectral segregation of the two sources.

FastICA/exact inversion are calibration baselines, not targets the arbor is expected to outperform on this easy gate.
