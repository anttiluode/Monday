# Transfer-ratio view

For one soma, overall complex scale is irrelevant to source purity.  The effective morphology can therefore be summarized at one carrier by the complex ratio

`r = H2 / H1`.

For mixing matrix

`A = [[a, b], [c, d]]`,

target source 1 is isolated when

`b + r*d = 0`, hence `r* = -b/d` (assuming `H1 != 0`, `d != 0`).

This is an especially sharp diagnostic.  Sample or mutate legal geometries and ask whether their measured `H2/H1` can approach the required ratio.  If not, Gate 0 is impossible with the current material/mutation vocabulary.  If yes, failure becomes a search/credit problem.
